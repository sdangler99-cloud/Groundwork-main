Privacy Policy

Groundwork respects your privacy. Journal entries, gratitude lists, and your color theme preference are stored locally on your device using your browser's storage. This information is never transmitted to Steve Dangler, Groundwork, or any third party — there is no account, login, or cloud sync.

Location data used in the Resources tab is used only to build search links to third-party directories (like SAMHSA's treatment locator or Psychology Today) and is not stored or transmitted anywhere by Groundwork itself.

If you use Send Suggestions, whatever you write is sent directly through your device's own email app to Steve Dangler's inbox — it isn't stored by Groundwork first.

Clearing your browser data, uninstalling the app, or switching devices will permanently delete all locally stored information unless you've exported it first from the Your Data section below.
